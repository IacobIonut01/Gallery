## What's new in 5.1.1
ReFra 5.1.1 makes it easier to capture the perfect moment, connect securely to Nextcloud, search more of your library, and keep browsing, backup, and background work fast and reliable.

### New features
- **Motion Photo and video frame extraction** — Scrub to an exact moment, select one or more frames, and export up to 50 JPEG or PNG images.
- **Nextcloud two-factor sign-in** — Authorize in your browser with Login Flow v2, including two-factor authentication, without giving ReFra your account password.
- **Search ignored albums** — Optionally include timeline-hidden albums in Smart Features and Smart Search while locked albums remain private.
- **Remote and keyboard navigation** — Move predictably through settings with a D-pad, keyboard, or remote and clear focus highlighting.

### Improvements
- **Resumable Smart Features scans** — Checkpointed, energy-aware stages preserve valid work, continue after interruption, and skip already-current results.
- **Faster thumbnail grids** — Lightweight thumbnails keep scrolling smooth, then refine to full quality when the grid stops.
- **More efficient cloud backups** — Streamed uploads, bounded parallelism, and preserved verification state reduce repeated I/O and network work.
- **Clearer timeline organization** — Configure date separators and daily, monthly, or yearly grouping independently for each gallery section.
- **Accurate status and guidance** — Setup recognizes limited photo access, Security reports active encryption fallbacks, and Help search discovers more settings.

### Bug fixes
- Large albums open the selected item, and merged albums browse and filter all their source folders.
- Motion Photos play reliably through zoom, rotation, paging, and lifecycle changes.
- Favorite, trash, restore, delete, and write actions work safely for unclassified formats such as JXL, JPEG 2000, PSD, and APNG.
- Cloud indexing notifications clear correctly, original downloads remain safe, and verified backup state survives re-indexing.
- Smart categories discard deleted media and refresh stale counts and cover thumbnails.
- Fixed trash completion, repeated empty face scans, search Back behavior, gallery error states, date-format overrides, viewer contrast and transitions, rotation lock, and pinch-grid bounds.
- Removed the obsolete automatic Subject Cutout suggestion pill while retaining editor and configured long-press access.
