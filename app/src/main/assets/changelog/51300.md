## What's new in 5.1.3
ReFra 5.1.3 makes browsing and organizing media faster and safer, while improving editing, location indexing, offline AI models, and thumbnail accuracy.

### New features
- **Instant tap navigation** — Optionally tap either edge of the media viewer to move immediately between photos and videos.
- **Safe moves from restricted folders** — Copy or move media from folders such as WhatsApp storage through a verified transfer with automatic rollback when needed.

### Improvements
- **Safer offline AI models** — Offline builds protect bundled models from deletion and unavailable network actions, with more reliable atomic installation.

### Bug fixes
- **Reliable editor markup and previews** — Blur, mosaic, highlighter, text, and borders survive save and reopen operations without black preview flashes or lost pending edits.
- **Preserved GPS metadata** — Bulk scans keep valid coordinates when address lookup fails and request unredacted location data when permitted.
- **Correct front-camera thumbnails** — Mirrored embedded JPEG thumbnails are bypassed so timeline previews match the full photo.
- Copied and moved media retain their original dates, and incomplete or declined transfers no longer leave duplicate files.
- The native AVIF and HEIF encoder build now uses the pinned NDK Clang toolchain for AOM assembly on F-Droid ARM builds.
