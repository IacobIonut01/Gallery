## What's new in 5.0.2

Editor and viewer refinements, plus more reliable manual backups.

### Improvements
- **Editor fixes** — Markup text stays within the canvas, the crop box resizes from its handles without auto-zooming, and the adjustment scrubber keeps the value you set.
- **Smoother media viewer** — Swipe-to-dismiss triggers naturally, a second swipe no longer closes the app, the rotate button works after multiple turns, and the top bar and quick actions respond correctly while the info sheet is open.
- **Reliable manual backup** — Start backup and Upload now work even when Auto-sync is turned off.

### Bug fixes
- Fixed grouped video overlapping the controls and stretching when switching members, and View all metadata for photos opened from other apps.
