## What's new in 4.0.0

A ground-up refresh: maps, redesigned search and selection, and a polished interface.

### New features
- **Location browsing** — Browse your photos on an interactive map by location.
- **Custom album thumbnails** — Set any photo as the cover for your albums.
- **Redesigned selection sheet** — Revamped selection actions with addons and select-all.
- **Redesigned search** — Rebuilt search experience with improved results and history.
- **List-based albums** — View your albums in a compact list layout.
- **Lock images** — Lock the current photo to prevent accidental swipes.

### Improvements
- **Improved vault playback** — Fixed encrypted video streaming for reliable playback.
- **UI refresh** — Polished interface with refined animations and layouts.
