## What's new in 4.2.1

Casting, an auto-contrast beta and a vault overhaul.

### New features
- **FCast video casting** — Cast videos and images to devices on your local network.
- **Auto-contrast (Beta)** — Real-time luminance-based contrast enhancement in the viewer.
- **Right-align selection actions** — Move top action buttons to the right for easier one-handed use.
- **Vault overhaul** — Gate authentication, custom passwords, per-vault security, and a streamlined flow.
- **Offline WithML variant** — New build variant with AI models but without online features (maps, cloud, network).

### Bug fixes
- Fixed phantom selections, corrected setup permissions, and improved image loading.
