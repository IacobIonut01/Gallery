## What's new in 4.3.0

Story cards, redesigned video controls and a much faster cold start.

### New features
- **Story cards** — Swipeable photo stories above the timeline with auto-advancing viewer and configurable card types.
- **Redesigned video controls** — Animated more-options popup replaces the tall stack of video control buttons.
- **Video auto-contrast** — Auto-contrast and adaptive colors now extend to the video options popup.

### Improvements
- **Faster cold startup** — Cold startup optimized from ~900 ms to ~350 ms with lazy keystore caching and deferred initialization.
- **Improved shared element transitions** — More accurate photo-to-viewer transitions with single stable registration and Long-based keys.

### Bug fixes
- Fixed move/copy album access with MANAGE_MEDIA, screen rotation reset on video exit, status bar sync, ClassCastException after unlock, grid realignment on back navigation, loading shimmer during startup, and empty-media padding.
