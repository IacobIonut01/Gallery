## What's new in 5.1.0
ReFra 5.1.0 brings your local and remote libraries together, adds powerful on-device creative tools, and makes large, animated and HDR photos faster and sharper.

### New features
- **Multi-account cloud library** — Connect Immich, ownCloud, Nextcloud, WebDAV, SMB and NFS, choose backup destinations, monitor sync and keep media available offline.
- **On-device subject cutout** — Detect, refine, copy, share or save a subject with a transparent background, without uploading the photo.
- **People and face blur** — Scan and group faces locally, browse people, and blur detected faces in the editor.
- **RAW development** — Adjust RAW white balance, exposure, highlights, colour and demosaicing, then export JPEG, PNG or TIFF.
- **Full-resolution editing** — A tiled, memory-bounded pipeline preserves resolution and source format on large photos.
- **HDR HEIC viewing** — Native tiled decoding and Ultra HDR gain maps provide sharp, memory-safe zoom on supported displays.
- **Interactive photo map** — Browse thumbnail clusters, switch map appearance and open a unified location timeline.
- **Lossless metadata sanitization** — Remove sensitive metadata from supported images without recompressing their pixels.
- **Albums** — Play configurable slideshows, browse media-type albums and choose custom album covers.
- **Secure picker vaults** — Authenticate individual vaults and select safely across them.

### Improvements
- **Help & Tips** — Search all guidance with fuzzy matching, open quick actions and view real UI previews.
- **System-aware dates** — Date and time formats follow the device by default, with guidance for custom patterns.
- **Viewer quality** — GIF, animated WebP and APNG render at full resolution, while opening and swiping are smoother.
- **Pinned albums** — Choose tile or banner layouts with privacy-safe locked thumbnails.
- **Selection and navigation** — More accurate drag selection, stable counters and tap-again scroll-to-top navigation.

### Bug fixes
- Fixed images incorrectly identified as RAW or TIFF, plus JXL metadata and SVG rendering issues.
- Fixed private-folder move, delete, playback and refresh failures.
- Fixed editor save failures, stale previews and streamed overwrite safety.
- Fixed subject-cutout gesture, paging and cleanup issues.
- Fixed mosaic and smart-category grid crashes.
- Fixed picker back navigation and multi-account cloud selection actions.
