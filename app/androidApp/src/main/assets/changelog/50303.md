## What's new in 5.0.3

A polish release focused on video metadata, faster private folders and a smoother viewer.

### New features
- **Richer video metadata** — iPhone HEVC .MOV files now show full QuickTime and EXIF details — camera make and model, lens, focal length and dates — instead of an empty sheet.
- **Faster private folders** — Private folder media now fills in progressively as it is discovered instead of staying blank until the whole folder is scanned.
- **Fix black video on hide** — A new Video Playback toggle re-binds the player surface so videos no longer black out when the system bars are shown or hidden (on by default for Samsung).

### Improvements
- **Smoother viewer** — Paddings, the info sheet and video controls adapt correctly on rotation, the timeline keeps its scroll position when you return, and the info panel no longer auto-hides mid-drag or gets stuck invisible.
- **Editor fixes** — Crop no longer zooms when dragged out of bounds, and markup hangs, undo of no-op adjustments, the rotate button and off-canvas text have all been fixed.

### Bug fixes
- Fixed hiding items to a vault from the selection sheet, View all metadata for images opened from other apps, a stuck pull-to-refresh spinner, the material navigation toggle flashing the screen, and secure camera reviews over the lockscreen.
