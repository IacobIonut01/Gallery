## What's new in 4.2.3

Timeline filtering, richer video playback and a smarter search.

### New features
- **Timeline filter** — Filter the timeline by media type, favorites, year, or album.
- **Animated format support** — Play animated AVIF, JXL, and APNG images alongside GIF.
- **Video subtitles** — Embedded and external subtitle track selection with bottom-sheet management.
- **Video pinch-to-zoom** — Pinch-to-zoom and double-tap-to-zoom during video playback.
- **Enhanced search** — Search through image descriptions, metadata text fields, and the inline timeline filter.
- **Album picker search** — Search bar in the copy/move album picker for quick album lookup.

### Improvements
- **Redesigned Trash & Favorites** — Refreshed layout, improved visual hierarchy, and smoother animations.
- **UX improvements** — Dismissible settings banners, bottom-sheet confirmations, and optimistic trash feedback.

### Bug fixes
- Fixed drag-selection tracking, metadata edit keyboard overlap, and preserved original dates when overriding edited photos.
