## What's new in 5.0.1

More image formats, faster decoding and steadier scrolling.

### New features
- **More image formats** — View PSD, JPEG 2000, TIFF, and SVG files in the timeline and viewer, each with crisp pinch-to-zoom.
- **Use system font** — A new display option switches the app over to your device's system font instead of the bundled typeface.

### Improvements
- **Faster HEIC & AVIF** — Hardware-accelerated HEIC/AVIF decoding with fixed AVIF color artifacts.
- **High-quality JXL zoom** — JPEG XL images now zoom sharply in the viewer through a dedicated region decoder.
- **Smoother video playback** — Video follows the media volume stream, zoom and pinch behave naturally on rotation, and the info section stays open when auto-hide fires.
- **Stable scrolling & navigation** — Timeline and settings scroll positions are preserved, and viewer exit transitions no longer break.

### Bug fixes
- Improved editor markup gestures and crop box interactions, fixed launch crash on null MediaStore columns, black screen after deleting in the standalone viewer, panorama compass and gyro on rotation, album cover mismatch, date-field text, scrubber near zero, widget self-healing, and updated Simplified Chinese translations.
