Deleting does not erase straight away. When the trash is enabled, deleted photos and videos move to **Library › Trash** and stay recoverable for a retention period controlled by Android and your media provider. For device files that is normally 30 days, and the trash screen tells you the expectation up front.

Trash requires Android 11 or later; on older versions, deletion is always immediate. When the feature cannot apply to an item, the app warns you first: "Some items cannot be trashed. Such items will be deleted permanently instead."

## Open the trash

<PreviewCollection feature="trash" section="open-the-trash" />

1. Tap **Library**.
2. Tap the **Trash** tile; its counter shows how many items it holds.
3. The grid lists everything trashed, with the usual gestures.

At the bottom, two buttons act on the whole trash: **Restore all** puts every item back where it came from, and **Delete all permanently** empties the trash. That one is irreversible and asks you to confirm.

## Restore or delete individual items

1. In Trash, touch and hold an item, then tap or drag across others.
2. Tap **Restore** to return them to their original albums, or **Delete permanently** to erase them now.
3. Both actions ask for confirmation; permanent deletion cannot be undone.

Restored items reappear in their folder and on the timeline in their original position.

## Turn the trash off

1. Open **Settings › General**.
2. Switch off **Use trash can**.
3. From then on, deleting is immediate and irreversible. The setting's own text says so.

A separate switch, **Require confirmation on trash**, adds a dialog before items move to the trash; it only applies while the trash is on. The same General page has an **Open Trash** shortcut to review, restore, or permanently delete items.

## Cloud media

Deleting an item that lives on a cloud account follows that provider's rules. Providers that support a server-side trash keep the item recoverable there; providers that do not delete permanently. The app tells you which applies before you confirm. A read-only account cannot delete at all, so the action is not offered.

## Example: recover a deleted shot

1. You deleted a photo from **Camera** yesterday.
2. Open **Library › Trash** and find it near the top.
3. Touch and hold it, tap **Restore**, and confirm.
4. The photo is back in **Camera** and back in the timeline on its original date.

## If deletion skipped the trash

- **The item was not trashable.** Some sources cannot hold trash, and the app warned that the deletion would be permanent.
- **Trash is off.** Check **Settings › General › Use trash can**.
- **It was a cloud item.** Retention follows the provider, not the 30-day device window.
- **Items vanished from Trash.** The retention period expired; expired items are gone for good.
