Cloud albums appear in their own section on the **Albums** tab, headed by the provider and account name, with a shared badge on albums shared with you. Opening one shows the remote media like any album.

<PreviewCollection feature="cloud-albums" section="browse-cloud-albums" />

## The menu depends on your access

Inside a cloud album, the three-dot menu offers only what your account can do:

- **Rename album** and **Delete album** appear only on albums your account owns.
- **Remove from album** appears for your selection when you own the album or have editor rights on a shared one.
- A read-only shared album offers none of these; you can browse and copy from it but not change it.
- If the account's own [read-only mode](/ReFra/features/cloud-settings) is on, actions like export and share are withheld from cloud items regardless of your server-side rights; that is a local safety switch, not the server refusing you.

On **Immich** these actions talk to the server directly: renaming renames the remote album, removing takes the items out of that album, and deleting removes the album itself. None of those delete the photos: they remain in the Immich library. Deleting a *photo* is a different action and does remove the asset.

Folder-style providers (WebDAV, SMB, NFS) let you browse, copy, and move files in and out of their folders where permissions allow, but do not present Immich-style album membership controls.

## Example: tidy a shared Immich album

1. A family member shared the "Holidays 2024" album with your Immich account and gave you editor rights.
2. Open it from the cloud section on Albums, select the photos that do not belong, and use **Remove from album**; they stay in the owner's library, just out of this album.
3. Rename is not on your menu; only the owner sees **Rename album** and **Delete album**. If you need it renamed, ask the owner.
