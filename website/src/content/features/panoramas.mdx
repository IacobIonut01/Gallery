Wide panoramas and spherical photo spheres open in the normal viewer, rendered through a panoramic projection so you can look around inside the image instead of seeing it squashed flat.

## Looking around

<PreviewCollection feature="panoramas" section="looking-around" />

- **Drag** to look left, right, up, and down within the scene.
- **Pinch** to zoom in on a detail and back out.
- **Tap** to show or hide the viewer controls, as on any photo.
- On a photo the camera recognized as a full photo sphere, turning the phone steers the view with the gyroscope; a compass-style indicator shows which way you are facing.

A merely wide photo is not automatically treated as a sphere; the app uses the projection that matches how the image was captured. There is no projection picker or VR mode; the viewing experience is what you see.

Viewing never changes the file: panning and zooming are on screen only, and the original stays as shot.

## Example: walk a panorama end to end

1. Open the wide panorama from your trip.
2. Drag slowly from one end to the other, then pinch in on a face or sign you want to read.
3. Tap once to bring the controls back when you are done.
