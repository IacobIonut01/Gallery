A slideshow plays through an album's photos and videos, advancing on its own while you watch. It starts from inside an album, so you always know exactly which set is playing.

## Start a slideshow

1. Open **Albums** and tap the album you want to watch.
2. Tap the **slideshow icon** in the album's top bar.
3. The **Slideshow options** sheet opens with the settings below.
4. Adjust them if needed and tap **Start**. The viewer switches to full-screen playback.

The sheet's **More settings** link opens the same options under **Settings › Media Viewer › Slideshow**, so changes you make in either place apply to the next slideshow.

## While it plays

<PreviewCollection feature="slideshow" section="while-it-plays" />

- The controls show **Previous**, **Play / Pause**, **Next**, and **Exit slideshow**.
- Tap to reveal the controls if they have hidden themselves.
- Photos stay for the chosen interval and advance; videos play through and the slideshow moves on when each ends.

## Options

<PreviewCollection feature="slideshow" section="options" />

| Option | Choices | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Interval** | one to 60 seconds | How long each photo stays on screen before advancing. Videos play through once; the interval does not cut them off. |
| **Transition** | **Slide**, **Fade**, **Ken Burns** | How one item gives way to the next. |
| **Ken Burns effect** | on/off | Slowly zooms photos while they are shown. |
| **Shuffle** | on/off | Plays items in random order. Shuffling is incompatible with **Reverse order**, so turning it on takes precedence. |
| **Reverse order** | on/off | Plays the set backwards; unavailable while **Shuffle** is on. |
| **Loop** | on/off | Restarts from the beginning when the set ends. |
| **Include GIFs** | on/off | Whether animated images take part. |
| **Include videos** | on/off | Whether videos play within the slideshow. |

Animated transitions and the Ken Burns zoom depend on **Animate media items** under **Settings › Appearance**; turning that off suppresses them.

## Example: the family album on the table

1. Open **Albums › Holiday 2026**.
2. Tap the slideshow icon in the top bar.
3. In the options sheet, set **Interval** to five seconds, **Transition** to **Fade**, **Shuffle** on, and **Loop** off.
4. Tap **Start**. The album plays once through in a random order, five seconds a photo.

## If the controls do not appear

- Tap the screen once; the controls hide during playback and come back on a tap.
- Videos that seem to stall the slideshow are subject to **Include videos**; turn it off to keep a photo-only flow.
- The slideshow entry is on the album screen itself; there is no separate entry from the open media viewer.
