ReFra keeps a running record of what it does (screens visited, tasks run, errors) meant for reporting bugs and diagnosing problems. It is hidden by default and most people never need it.

## Unlock the Developer section

1. Open **Settings**.
2. **Touch and hold** the version label in the app header at the top. (If the banner was moved to the bottom, hold the version shown in the compact bar there.)
3. The **Developer** row appears in the hub.

## The Developer screen

The top shows a diagnostics summary: sessions recorded, entries captured, size, and crashes. If a crash log from an earlier session was never reported, a banner offers to **Report a bug** or **Dismiss** it.

- **Session logs**: browses every captured entry.
- **Latest errors**: the most recent errors for a quick check.
- **Report a bug**, **Share zip**, **Copy logs**, **Clear**: export or wipe the logs. Clearing asks first; every recorded session and crash log is deleted.
- **Minimum log level**: entries below the chosen level are discarded at capture time.
- **Record breadcrumbs**: records the screens and actions leading up to an error, which makes a report much more useful.
- **Log a test error**: writes a synthetic error so you can see what one looks like.
- **Disable developer mode**: stops collection and deletes every log, after confirmation.

## Read the session logs

<PreviewCollection feature="session-logs" section="read-the-session-logs" />

1. Open **Settings › Developer › Session logs**.
2. Switch between **By area** (entries grouped by the part of the app that wrote them, a screen or a background task such as an upload) and **Recent** (a flat newest-first list).
3. Filter with the **Search logs** field, the severity chips (**verbose**, **debug**, **info**, **warn**, **error**, **crash**, **event**, roughly "routine detail" up to "something broke"), and the time-range chips: **1h**, **24h**, **7d**, **All**, or a custom **Range**.
4. Tap an entry for its detail sheet: the message, its **Context** (extra details the app recorded alongside it), the **Stack trace** (the chain of code locations when an error occurred) when there is one, and the **Breadcrumbs** (the screens and actions leading up to it). **Copy** takes it to the clipboard and **Report** attaches it to a bug report.

## Privacy

Logs exist so problems can be explained, and they can contain file names, paths, and server addresses. Review what you are sending before sharing a zip or pasting entries into a report.

## Example: report a crash

1. After the app crashes, reopen it and go to **Settings › Developer**. The crash banner appears.
2. Tap **Report a bug**, or open **Session logs** and find the error entry for details.
3. **Share zip** to send the whole log bundle, or **Copy** the single entry.
4. If you are done diagnosing, **Clear** or disable developer mode to wipe everything.
