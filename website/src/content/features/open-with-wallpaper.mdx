Two handoffs connect ReFra to the rest of the system: opening files other apps point at, and sending a photo to your wallpaper tool.

## Open with ReFra

From a file manager, browser download, or chat, use Android's **Open with** and choose ReFra. The file opens in the viewer even if it is not part of the indexed library. The grant is read-only by nature: actions like **Edit**, **Move**, **Rename**, and **Delete** can be missing on these items because the app was not given write access to the file. **Share** and viewing still work.

## Use as wallpaper

<PreviewCollection feature="open-with-wallpaper" section="use-as-wallpaper" />

1. Open the photo, tap **More options › Use as**, and pick the wallpaper handler Android offers.
2. The crop and set screen belongs to that handler; ReFra hands over the image and the wallpaper app finishes the job.
3. If no handler is installed, the action reports that no app can do it; installing any wallpaper-capable app restores the path.

## More editors

The viewer's **Open with** and the editor's **More** tab both route to other installed apps; pick your favorite editor there when the built-in tools are not enough.

## Example: a downloaded image as wallpaper

1. In your file manager, open the downloaded PNG with ReFra.
2. **More options › Use as** and choose the wallpaper option.
3. Crop in the handler's screen and confirm; the file itself is unchanged.
