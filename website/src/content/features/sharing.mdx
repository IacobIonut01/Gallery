Sharing hands the actual files to Android's share sheet, so whatever apps are installed (messaging, email, social, cloud storage) can receive them. ReFra hands the file to the chosen app. That app controls how it is sent and may reduce its quality.

## Share a single item

<PreviewCollection feature="sharing" section="share-a-single-item" />

1. Tap a photo or video to open it in the viewer.
2. Tap the **share icon** in the bottom bar (the three connected dots).
3. The Android share sheet slides up; pick the app or contact to send to.

## Share several items at once

1. On the Timeline or in an album, **touch and hold** the first item, then tap or drag across the rest.
2. Tap **Share** in the action sheet.
3. All selected items go to the chosen app as one bundle. How many files an app accepts in one share depends on that app.

## Copy to clipboard instead

<PreviewCollection feature="sharing" section="copy-to-clipboard-instead" />

When you want to paste a photo rather than send it:

1. Open the photo.
2. Tap the **copy icon** in the action bar, or use **Copy to clipboard** where offered.
3. Switch to a messaging app, document, or email and paste.

This copies the image itself, not an album reference. How long the clipboard keeps it is up to Android and the app you paste into.

## What gets shared

- **File quality**: ReFra passes the original file to the receiving app. That app decides what to do with it and may compress it before sending.
- **Cloud items**: sharing an item that only exists on a cloud account downloads it first so the other app receives real file data.
- **Hidden metadata**: photos can carry location and camera details inside the file. If you want to remove location or other details, prepare a copy with **Remove metadata** before sharing it; see [metadata](/ReFra/features/metadata).

## Example: send holiday photos to a chat

1. Open **Albums › Holiday 2026**.
2. Touch and hold the first photo, then tap five more.
3. Tap **Share**, pick your messaging app, and choose the conversation.
4. Review the attachments in that app and send.

## If sharing does nothing

- The receiving app sets the limit. Some apps refuse large batches or videos over a size cap; try fewer items.
- A share that stalls on a cloud-only item is usually the download. Wait for it or retry on a better connection.
- Paste targets are the receiving app's choice. An app with no image paste field cannot take a clipboard photo.
