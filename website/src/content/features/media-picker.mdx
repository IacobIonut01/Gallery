When another app asks Android for a photo or video (attaching to a message, uploading to a form), ReFra can answer instead of the system picker, but only where Android offers the choice; ReFra does not take over other apps' pickers on its own. What you may pick (photos only, videos only, one or many) is set by the calling app; the picker just honors the request.

## Pick media for another app

<PreviewCollection feature="media-picker" section="pick-media-for-another-app" />

1. In the other app, choose the attach/open action and pick ReFra when Android offers the choice.
2. The picker opens with **Timeline** and **Albums** tabs; browse and tap items to select them; the count shows as you go.
3. **Preview** checks the selection; **Add** (or **Add N**) sends it back to the calling app. **Close** cancels without sending anything.

## Sources and limits

- Locked albums ask for their authentication before opening; private-folder, cloud, and vault content can be picked where offered.
- Cloud items download first and vault items decrypt to a temporary copy; that preparation runs after you tap **Add**, shown as **Preparing N of M…**, and reports **Some items could not be prepared** if any fail.
- Whatever you send becomes a readable copy inside the receiving app; a vault item does not stay encrypted once another app has it, so decide that before tapping **Add**.

## Example: attach a vault photo to a message

1. In the chat app, choose attach and pick ReFra when Android offers it.
2. Open the vault entry, unlock it, select the photo, and tap **Add**. The chat will receive a readable copy, not an encrypted file.
3. Wait for **Preparing** to finish; the chat opens its composer with the photo attached.
