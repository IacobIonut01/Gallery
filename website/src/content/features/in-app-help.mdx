**Settings › Help & tips** is the app's built-in manual: it is bundled with the app, so everything here works offline.

## Finding help

<PreviewCollection feature="in-app-help" section="finding-help" />

- The search field at the top looks across **Tips**, **Settings**, **Actions**, and **Changelog** entries, so "trash" or "pinch" finds the relevant card wherever it lives.
- Sections group the content: **Get started**, **Make the most of Gallery**, and **Explore more tips** lead to categorized tutorials (The basics, Navigation, Personalization, Timeline & album settings, Photo & video viewing, Media viewer actions, and more).
- A tutorial shows its steps and, where a relevant screen exists, a **Quick actions** or **Open settings** jump that takes you to it; some tips have no action at all.
- **What's new** lists the release notes shipped with your version, so what you read matches the build you run.

## The settings banner and support

The banner at the top of Settings carries **Donate** and **GitHub** buttons. Donate opens a support sheet listing the payment channels the project accepts (PayPal, Revolut, and crypto options); tapping a crypto entry copies its address to the clipboard; the app never initiates a payment, you finish it in your own wallet app. The banner can be collapsed to the bottom of the page and restored from there; see [the settings hub](/ReFra/features/settings-overview).

## Example: learn a gesture you keep forgetting

1. **Settings › Help & tips**, search "select".
2. Open the selection tutorial and read its steps.
3. Use the offered **Quick actions** or **Open settings** jump (if shown) to reach the related options and adjust them.
