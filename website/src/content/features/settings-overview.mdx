The Settings hub is a single list reached from **Library**, then the gear icon at the top right. Each row opens a section page; the guides below cover every row inside them.

## Open Settings

1. Tap **Library** in the bottom bar.
2. Tap the **gear icon** at the top right.

## The header banner

The top of the hub shows the app name and version plus **Donate** and **GitHub** buttons. Tapping the banner offers **Move to bottom**, which collapses it into a compact bar at the bottom of the page; tapping that compact bar offers **Move to top** to restore it. Touch-and-hold on the version label unlocks the hidden Developer section (see [session logs](/ReFra/features/session-logs)).

## Reading the hub

- Each section row shows a summary line of what it contains, so the list itself is a rough index.
- Sections control the app only. Device-level access (media permission, notifications, battery) lives in Android's own app settings; see [first launch](/ReFra/features/first-launch) for where the app defers to the system.
- Rows that belong to a feature sit inside that feature's section, so a missing option usually means its parent section, not a different one.

## Example: find and change a setting

1. You want the navigation bar to stop hiding while you scroll. Open **Library › gear icon**.
2. Tap **Navigation** and turn **Hide navigation bar on scroll** off.
3. Go back to the timeline; the bar now stays put while scrolling.
