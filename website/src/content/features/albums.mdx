The Albums tab groups your media by where it lives: each device folder that contains photos or videos (Camera, Screenshots, Downloads, WhatsApp, and so on) and each album on a connected cloud account.

## Open the Albums tab

Tap **Albums** in the bottom navigation bar. The screen shows, top to bottom:

1. **Pinned albums**: a row at the top holding albums you pinned; it only appears once you pin something.
2. **All albums**: the grid or list of folders and cloud albums. Each album shows a cover thumbnail and item count; cloud albums sit in their own section with the provider's icon, and albums others shared with you carry a "Shared" badge.
3. **Collections**: your virtual collections and a **New collection** tile. See [collections](/ReFra/features/collections).
4. **Media types**: virtual albums that collect all photos, all videos, all GIFs, or all RAW files across folders.

An album with a small lock icon is locked: opening it asks for your fingerprint or PIN first. See [locked albums](/ReFra/features/hidden-content).

## Browse and find an album

<PreviewCollection feature="albums" section="browse-and-find-an-album" />

- **Grid or list**: the view toggle switches between a thumbnail grid and a compact list. Pinch the grid to change its column count.
- **Sort**: the sort control orders albums by **Date** (recent activity first) or **Name**.
- **Inside an album**: tap an album to open it; its media appears in a date-grouped grid with the same gestures as the timeline. Pinch to resize, touch and hold to select, tap the check circle on a date header to select that group.

## Album actions

<PreviewCollection feature="albums" section="album-actions" />

Touch and hold any album to open its actions:

- **Pin / Unpin**: keep the album in the pinned row at the top.
- **Change album cover**: pick any photo as the cover instead of the most recent one.
- **Add to group**: bundle albums under a named, collapsible group.
- **Move to section**: override which collapsible section the album sits in.
- **Merge subfolders**: fold a folder's subfolders into a single album view; **Unmerge subfolders** splits them back.
- **Lock album / Unlock album**: require authentication to open it.
- **Add to ignored albums**: start an ignore rule for the folder; the follow-up sheet asks whether it hides from the Timeline, Albums, or both, without deleting files. See [ignored albums](/ReFra/features/ignored-albums).
- **Move album to trash / Delete album permanently**: removes the album's contents; the permanent option cannot be undone.

Groups, sections, covers, and merging are covered step by step in [album organization](/ReFra/features/album-organization).

## Example: find the Screenshots folder fast

1. Open **Albums**.
2. Touch and hold **Screenshots**, then tap **Pin**.
3. Screenshots now sits in the pinned row at the very top, whatever the sort order.
4. Tap the sort control and choose **Name** to alphabetize the rest of the grid.

## If an album is missing

- Its folder may have no media, or it may be covered by an ignore rule scoped to the Albums tab. Check **Library › Ignored**.
- The same folder name on two storage locations can appear as one album if **Merge albums by name** is on under **Settings › Timeline & Albums**.
- New folders appear after Android indexes them; pull down to refresh.
