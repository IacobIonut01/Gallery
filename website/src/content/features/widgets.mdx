ReFra ships two Android home-screen widgets: **Single Photo** (one photo on the home screen) and **Photo Grid** (a grid of photos, laid out 2×2, 4×4, or 6×6 depending on the size you give it).

## Add a widget

1. On your home screen, touch and hold an empty area and choose **Widgets**, then find ReFra.
2. Pick **Single Photo** or **Photo Grid**. The configuration screen opens.
3. **Select Photo** (or **Select Photos** for the grid) picks through the usual picker.
4. Under **Widget style**, choose **Original photo**, **Black & white**, or **Icon or emoji**; the last shows a text field for your icon with suggestion chips, and the icon replaces the photo while tapping the widget still opens it.
5. The preview shows the result; **Add widget** places it. The launcher decides the size, and you can resize the widget afterward like any other.

## Behavior

<PreviewCollection feature="widgets" section="behavior" />

- Tapping the widget opens the selected photo in ReFra.
- The **Black & white** style is a display effect stored with the widget; the original file is never altered.
- Reconfiguring an existing widget works where your launcher supports it; otherwise remove it and add a new one.
- Removing a widget from the home screen deletes only the widget; the photos stay in the library.

Pick photos you are comfortable showing on a home screen; a widget displays them to anyone holding the phone, without an unlock step in front of the picture itself.

## Example: a boarding-pass photo within reach

1. Add the **Single Photo** widget, select the screenshot of your boarding pass.
2. Keep **Original photo** style and **Add widget**.
3. At the gate, tap the widget to open it full screen.
