## In the app

**Settings › Help & tips › What's new** lists the notes bundled with the installed app. They normally match your build; for anything they do not cover, check the release notes for your installed version on the web.

## On the web

The [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/IacobIonut01/ReFra/releases) page carries the notes for every published version along with its downloads.

## Stable versus nightly

Stable releases carry full notes. Nightly releases are test builds; check the published date and notes for each one, because the latest nightly can be older than the latest stable and can behave worse rather than better.

## When a guide disagrees

If this documentation describes an option your version does not show, check the release notes for your installed version.
