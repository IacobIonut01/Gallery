## Report a problem

File bugs and feature requests on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/IacobIonut01/ReFra/issues). A useful report includes:

- The **app version**, shown at the top of the Settings page.
- Your **Android version**, from Android's settings.
- The steps that reproduce it; numbered steps work better than descriptions.
- A screenshot if the problem is visible. Note: if you enabled **Secure Mode**, screenshots of the app are blocked; describe what you see instead of turning it off.
- For crashes or sync problems, the in-app session logs can be attached (see [session logs](/ReFra/features/session-logs) for how to enable and share them).

## Ask the community

The [Telegram group](https://t.me/GalleryCompose) covers questions, previews, and release chatter. This is a community channel; answers come when someone knows them, not on a schedule.

## In-app help first

**Settings › Help & tips** inside the app answers most "where is" questions with tutorials and a searchable index; see [in-app help](/ReFra/features/in-app-help).

## Support the project

- The **Donate** button at the top of Settings lists the channels the project accepts; PayPal, Revolut, and several crypto networks. Tapping a crypto entry copies its address; the payment itself happens in your own app, never inside ReFra.
- Translate the app on [Crowdin](https://crowdin.com/project/gallery-compose).
- Report and vote on issues; well-described reports are direct help.
