ReFra is a gallery app for Android that shows the photos and videos it can access on your device, plus the libraries of any cloud accounts you connect, in one place. It is free and open source.

## The three sections

<PreviewCollection feature="main-navigation" section="the-navigation-bar" />

Everything in the app hangs off three destinations in the bottom navigation bar:

- **Timeline**: your photos and videos, newest first, grouped under date headers.
- **Albums**: the same media grouped by the folder or cloud album it lives in, plus your collections.
- **Library**: shortcuts and sections for Favorites, Trash, Vault, People, Locations, Categories, and your connected cloud accounts.

The bar stays at the bottom of the screen as you browse. Each tab remembers where you were, so you can leave a folder half-scrolled, check the timeline, and come back to the same spot.

## Finding things

Two tools cover most searches:

- **Search bar**: at the top of the Timeline. Tapping it opens search; type part of a file name, or, if the AI models are installed, describe the photo in words. See [search](/ReFra/features/search).
- **Filter**: the slider icon at the right of the search bar. It narrows the timeline by type, favorites, year, or album. See [timeline filters](/ReFra/features/timeline-filters).

## Settings and help

Tap **Library**, then the gear icon at the top right, to open Settings. Every option lives under one of its sections: Appearance, Timeline & Albums, Media Viewer, Navigation, General, Security, Backup & restore, Cloud Accounts, Smart Features, and Help & tips. The [find a setting](/ReFra/settings) page lists what each section holds.

The Help & tips section also contains the app's built-in guides, which stay available offline.

## Local and cloud media

ReFra treats device media and cloud media as one library. A photo stored only on your Nextcloud server appears in the timeline next to one shot on your phone, marked with a small cloud badge. Actions behave a little differently for remote items (downloading, for example), and each feature page notes the difference where it matters.

## Example: find a photo and share it

1. Open ReFra. You land on the **Timeline** (or whichever screen you set as your launch screen).
2. Scroll down to roughly when the photo was taken, or tap the search bar and type part of its file name.
3. Tap the thumbnail. The photo opens full screen.
4. Tap the share icon in the bottom bar (the arrow-and-node symbol) and pick the app to send it through.

ReFra hands the file to the chosen app. That app controls how it is sent and may reduce its quality.
